THE OBJECTIVE

Turn a social page into an active point of attention.

The goal was not simply to post more. It was to create content that gave the page a stronger presence, encouraged people to watch and interact, and created a path toward enquiries.

WHAT I DID

Content, creative decisions and execution.

Content direction

Planned short-form content around the page's visual identity and the kind of attention the audience could respond to.

Reel creation

Edited and produced the reels used during the 15-day period, with attention to pacing, hooks and visual presentation.

Social management

Managed the creative side of the page and looked at audience response to understand which content was gaining traction.

CONTENT HIGHLIGHTS

The work that went out.

THE RESULT

More attention. A clearer signal.

Within 15 days, video views moved from roughly 200 to around 2,000, the page gained followers, and the work generated one lead. The numbers show the immediate movement; the bigger takeaway was learning which creative choices could turn attention into action.
